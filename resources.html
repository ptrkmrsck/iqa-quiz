Resources

Maqam World & Inside Arabic Music

Maqam World
Reference for Arabic maqamat, ajnas, iqa‘at, instruments, and musical forms. The source of this app’s notation, reference pitches, and recordings.
Inside Arabic Music
Arabic Maqam Performance and Theory in the 20th Century, by Johnny Farraj and Sami Abu Shumays (Oxford University Press, 2019). A reference on Arabic music theory and performance, with Maqam World as its companion website. The linked page includes purchasing options.

Listening & maqam practice

Maqam Lessons — Ear Training Website
Sami Abu Shumays’s listening exercises compare ajnas, changes in scale degrees, and examples at different tonics.
Sameeny iPhone
Arabic maqam ear training, pitch discrimination, personalized tests, and progress tracking.
Altarab iPhone / iPad · Android
Maqam exploration and comparison, iqa playback and tap-along practice, solfège, and instrument tools.
Maqam Academy Android
Maqam scale playback, a quarter-tone keyboard, and singing or instrument practice with note feedback.
Bonjour Maqami iPhone / iPad
Listening quizzes for maqamat and ajnas, modulation recognition, and melodic dictation.
Maqam Notes Player Website
Playable notes, phrase looping, guided scale exercises, adjustable tempo, and a quiz mode. Available in English, Hebrew, and Arabic without an account.
Maqamat Al-Arabiya iPhone / iPad
Maqam families, constituent ajnas, intervals, and oud playback with repetition.
The Whole Horn Website / iOS / Android
Ear-training games including “Half-Flat” tuning exercises and “Maqam Map” recognition, with listening comparisons after incorrect answers.

Rhythm practice

Iqa3 iOS / Android
Arabic rhythm loops with tempo adjustment, offline playback, and background playback for instrument practice.
Arabic Rhythm Trainer Website
Looping iqa playback, adjustable tempo, dum/tak notation, and step-by-step counting.
Iqa’at: Arabic Rhythms iPhone / iPad
Over 80 rhythms with notation, adjustable tempo, looping, and several percussion instruments. Most patterns are presented in their basic skeleton form.
