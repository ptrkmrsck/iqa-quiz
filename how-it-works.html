How it works

Iqa practices rhythms. Jins practices the melodic building blocks of maqamat.

Identify Default

  1. Listen to the example or look at the notation.
  2. Choose the rhythm or jins name.
  3. Try again if you miss it. Select Next when you get it right.

Prefer to answer without choices? Select Recall in Settings, then reveal the answer to check yourself.

Perform

  1. Read the rhythm or jins name.
  2. Sing or play it from memory.
  3. Select Reveal to see the notation and hear an example.

For Jins, you can also tap the revealed notes or select Play reference notes. The app does not record or grade you.

Check yourself

In Perform and Recall, you decide how you did:

  • Got it — you recalled it without help.
  • Again — you needed help or want another attempt.

Missed items return during the round. At the end, Practice missed lets you review them together.

How rounds and repeats work

Again returns an item after up to three other questions, or sooner if fewer remain. With only one item selected, it returns immediately. Multiple-choice mistakes also put the item back in the round.

In Perform, either rating advances. In Recall, Got it reveals the remaining details, then Next advances.

Practice missed includes anything you missed during the round, even if you later got it right.

Settings reference
Practice direction
Switch between Identify and Perform while keeping the current item and form. Switching hides the answer again; your Identify settings are retained.
What you see and hear
In Identify, enable notation, audio, or both. Iqa audio can be None, Tabla demonstrations by Faisal Zedan, or Musical examples. Jins has independent Notation, Reference notes, and Musical examples toggles.
Answers
Multiple choice offers up to four names and needs at least two selected items. Recall uses Reveal answer followed by Again or Got it. Perform always uses self-assessment.
Selections and presets
Presets apply immediately; changing a checkbox makes the selection Custom. Fresh visits start with Common rhythms for Iqa and Foundations for Jins. Search filters the list without changing the selection. Random picks roughly the same number of eligible items (four if empty); All selects everything and Clear deselects everything.
Meter presets
Even & triple meters includes even beat counts plus 3/4 and 3/8. Odd meters includes odd counts greater than three. These use the time-signature numerator, so 10/8 is in Even & triple meters.
Restarting and changing settings
Restart round or a changed selection starts a new shuffled round and resets its counters. Changing cues keeps the current question. In Iqa Identify, items without the chosen recording type are excluded from new questions.
Sharing and saving
Copy selection link shares the selected items and settings, not your progress. Settings are stored in your browser without an account or cross-device synchronization.
Keyboard shortcuts
1–4 choose an answer; Space controls audio; Enter advances when Next is shown. Use Tab to reach other controls and Enter to activate them. Shortcuts are inactive in Settings or while editing a field.
Playback and musical details
Autoplay and looping
Iqa Identify’s Autoplay is off by default; Jins Identify starts manually. Perform plays a musical example on Reveal. If autoplay is blocked, press Play. Recordings loop until paused or you advance.
Jins notes and tone
Tap anywhere on the notation to hear the nearest note horizontally. Play reference notes plays the study outline, not every displayed note. Sine, Square, Sawtooth, and Triangle change the synthesized tone, not recordings. Notes and musical examples stop each other.
Forms and musical examples
Each question chooses a form and a matching recording. They stay fixed during retries but may change when the item returns. Some tempo variants have no separate transcription; these are labeled.
Comparing your performance
Any familiar form is fine. The recording is an example, not the only correct performance; it may include ornamentation or accompaniment.
Reference pitches
Jins playback uses Maqam World’s reference frequencies rather than equal temperament. These are study references, not universal tuning rules. Extended outlines and unspecified ghammaz roles are explained with the revealed answer.
Notation and connection
In Iqa notation, D = Dum, T = Tak, and s = Silence. Images and recordings need an internet connection to Maqam World. See Resources for further study.
