Practice

Iqa / Jins
Iqa practices rhythmic cycles. Jins practices melodic building blocks of maqamat; the plural of jins is ajnas.
Identify
The default direction. Use notation, audio, or both to name the rhythm or jins. Choose your cues and answer method in Settings.
Perform
See the name, then sing or play it. Select Reveal to show the transcription and automatically play a random musical example. For Jins, you can also tap the notation or play the reference notes. Nothing is recorded or automatically graded.
Multiple choice
Choose from up to four names. Wrong choices turn red and are disabled so you can try again. A correct answer reveals details and a Next button. Select at least two items to use multiple choice.
Recall
In Identify, name the rhythm or jins yourself, then select Reveal answer and assess yourself with Again or Got it.
Again / Got it
Again marks the item as missed and returns it after up to three other questions. If fewer remain, it returns sooner; with one selected item, it returns immediately. Got it completes that attempt. In Perform, either button advances; in Recall, Got it shows the remaining answer details before Next.
Rounds
Each eligible selected item appears once, with missed items returning for another attempt. Multiple-choice mistakes also put an item back in the round. The summary counts distinct items; Practice missed lets you revisit anything missed, even if you later got it right.

Settings

Practice direction
Switch between Identify and Perform without changing the current item or form. Switching hides the answer again. Identify’s cue and answer settings are retained while their controls are hidden in Perform.
Iqa: transcription and audio
Show or hide the transcription independently of audio. None disables recordings, Tabla uses Faisal Zedan’s demonstrations, and Musical examples uses other recordings. In Identify, enable at least one cue. Items without the chosen recording type are excluded from new questions.
Jins: question cues
Notation, Reference notes, and Musical examples are independent toggles in Identify. Enable any combination, with at least one cue. Names and tonic/ghammaz labels stay hidden until the answer is confirmed.
Presets and individual selections
Presets apply immediately. Add or remove items with the checkboxes; an edited preset becomes Custom selection. Iqa starts with Common rhythms and Jins starts with Foundations on a fresh visit.
Meter presets
Even & triple meters includes even beat counts plus 3/4 and 3/8. Odd meters includes odd beat counts greater than three. These use the numerator of the index time signature, so 10/8 belongs to Even & triple meters.
Search, Random, All, and Clear
Search filters the selection list without changing your selections. Random chooses a set of roughly the current size (four if empty), from eligible items. All selects the full catalog; Clear deselects everything.
Restart round
Starts a new shuffled round and resets its counters. Changing the selected items also starts a new round. Changing cues preserves the current question.
Copy selection link
Copies a link containing the selected items and practice settings, including direction and note tone. Anyone opening it can use the same setup. Settings are also stored in your browser; there is no account or cross-device synchronization.

Playback

Autoplay and looping
Iqa Identify has an Autoplay toggle, off by default. Jins Identify starts playback manually. Perform starts the musical example when you reveal. Recordings loop until paused or you advance; if your browser blocks autoplay, press Play.
Playable Jins notation
Tap anywhere on the image to hear the nearest note horizontally. All displayed notes are playable when the notation is shown, including before answering in Identify. Play reference notes plays the study outline rather than every displayed note.
Note sound
Choose Sine, Square, Sawtooth, or Triangle in Jins Settings. This changes synthesized reference notes and tapped notes, not musical recordings. Playing notes stops the recording, and playing the recording stops the notes.
Forms and examples
A form or version is chosen randomly per question, with a matching recording. It stays fixed during retries and cue changes, but may change when the item returns. Some tempo variants have no separate transcription; the app says so instead of showing a mismatched image.
Comparing your performance
In Perform, any familiar form is fine. The revealed recording is an example, not the only correct performance. Musical examples may include ornamentation or additional accompaniment.
Reference pitches
Jins notes use Maqam World’s reference frequencies, not a standard equal-temperament scale. They are a study reference, not a universal tuning prescription. Some ajnas need an extended outline or have no single defined ghammaz; these cases are labeled after the answer is revealed.
Notation key
For Iqa: D = Dum, T = Tak, and s = Silence.
Keyboard shortcuts
1–4 choose multiple-choice answers. Space controls the current recording or Jins reference-note playback. Enter advances when Next is shown. Tab moves between controls; Enter activates the focused button, including Reveal. Shortcuts are inactive while Settings is open or you are editing a field.
Internet connection
Notation and recordings load from Maqam World and require a connection. See Resources for the source website, book, and other learning tools.
